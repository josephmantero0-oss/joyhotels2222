📖 JoyHotels Staff Guide

🗝️ How to Check-In a Guest

1. Tap any Green (Available) room card.
2. Search First: Enter their phone number; if they've stayed before, their details will auto-fill.
3. Identity: Take a clear photo of their ID. You can upload multiple images (Front & Back).
4. Group Booking: If they need more rooms, select them in the "Additional Rooms" section before saving.

💳 Managing Payments & Checkout

1. Tap a Red (Occupied) room card to see the bill.
2. Extra Charges: Add food, laundry, or water charges as they occur during the stay.
3. Check-out: Verify all charges with the guest, then tap "Process Checkout". The room will automatically turn Yellow (Dirty).

🧹 Cleaning & Housekeeping

Rooms that turn Yellow (Dirty) are ready for cleaning. Housekeepers can tap the room card to change the status to "Cleaning..." and finally to "Clean" when the room is sanitized and ready for the next guest.

📊 Financials & Reports

Managers can use the Financials tab to view a visual breakdown of revenue vs expenses. Click "Detailed Reports" to export monthly or yearly Excel/PDF files for accounting.

📅 Reservation Calendar

Use the **Calendar** to plan for future arrivals. If a guest wants to change their date, you can click their name on the calendar to see options or drag their entry to a new date.
